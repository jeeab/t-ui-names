T-UI place names

Offline place-name search for the T-UI launcher on the LilyGo T-Deck. Your T-Deck downloads these automatically for any area you download maps for.

Want a whole region at once? Get it from the downloads page and unzip it into the root of the SD card.

USA: USGS GNIS (public domain). Europe and world cities: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0.
